Delete your {{ brand }} account

{{ brand }} app, published by {{ entity }} · Français

1. In the app

Open {{ brand }}, go to Me, then Delete my account, and confirm. The deletion is immediate.

2. Without the app

If you no longer have the app, write to {{ deletion_email }}:

  • from the email address of your {{ brand }} account: this is how we check the request really comes from you;
  • with the subject "Delete my {{ brand }} account".

We delete the account within 7 days at most and confirm by email.

What is deleted

  • Your account: email, name, password, profile picture, settings.
  • Your couple's space: messages, voice notes, photos, memories, dates, lists, game answers, coach conversations, call history and shared locations.
  • Stored files (photos and voice notes).

Your partner keeps their own account. They are told, and can start a new space.

What is kept

  • No data from your account is kept in our database after the deletion.
  • Server technical logs (IP address, date, requested page, without any content) are erased automatically after 14 days.
  • Your Bolingo Plus purchase history stays with Apple or Google, under their own rules. Deleting your account does not cancel the subscription: cancel it in your Apple or Google account settings.

Delete only some data

Without deleting your account, you can delete a message, a memory or a photo in the app, or stop sharing your location at any time. For any other request, write to {{ deletion_email }}.
